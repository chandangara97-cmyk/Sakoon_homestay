About the stay

Old Kalpa, quietly

The homestay sits above the newer town, on the road toward the old village and Chini Bungalow, with the Kinner Kailash range filling the window every morning. Rooms are simple, warm, and built around a shared kitchen where meals are cooked the way they're cooked in Kinnauri households — rajma, siddu, and apples from the trees outside.

We're a short walk from Narayan-Nagini temple and the start of several day walks around Roghi and Ropa. In season, the orchard is part of daily life here, not a backdrop.

Getting here

Kalpa sits just above Reckong Peo on NH5, roughly 8–9 hours by road from Shimla. Taxis and HRTC buses run to Reckong Peo; from there it's a short climb up to the homestay.
